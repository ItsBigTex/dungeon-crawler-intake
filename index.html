Don't build the person you wish you were. Tell me who you actually are. If you're an accountant who has never touched a weapon, that's what you are. If you're a mechanic who can rebuild an engine but can't run a mile, that's what you are. If you spend all your free time gaming and know an unreasonable amount about obscure fantasy lore, that's useful information.

The dungeon isn't going to give you the character you wish you were.

It's going to give you the character you actually are.
